# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Project:** Nightingale — Early Ransomware Detection for Hospitals

## 1. Parties

This Agreement is made between:

- **[Team — add names / entity]**, of [address] ("**the Team**"), the developers of the Nightingale prototype; and
- **[Clinical pilot partner — add hospital / health-system name]**, of [address] ("**the Partner**").

The Team and the Partner are each a "**Party**" and together "**the Parties**".

## 2. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a clinical evaluation of the Nightingale prototype, an early ransomware detection system for hospital device fleets. In that evaluation, the Team may share source code, detection logic, and product plans, and the Partner may share hospital operational information such as device inventories, network topology, and ward workflows. Each Party may disclose Confidential Information to the other only for the Purpose of **evaluating the prototype and, if the Parties agree, running a pilot**. This Agreement covers both directions of disclosure.

## 3. Definitions

**"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information a Party discloses to the other in connection with the Purpose, in any form, including:

1. Nightingale source code, detection model details, signal weights, thresholds, architecture, and roadmap;
2. business, funding, and product plans of the Team;
3. hospital operational data of the Partner, including device inventories, network diagrams, and security procedures;
4. the existence and content of discussions between the Parties.

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public without breach of this Agreement; (b) the receiving Party can show by written records it developed independently before or after disclosure, without use of the disclosing Party's information; (c) the receiving Party receives from a third party without restriction; or (d) the disclosing Party approves in writing for release.

## 4. Obligations

Each Party will:

1. **Keep it confidential.** Use the other Party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose, and not disclose it to anyone else without prior written permission.
2. **Limit access.** Share Confidential Information only with employees, students, or advisers who need it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement. Each Party is responsible for breaches by its people.
3. **Protect it.** Use at least the same care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care.
4. **Minimise data.** **No real patient data may be entered into, connected to, or processed by the demo system.** The demonstration fleet is simulated data only. Any pilot involving real hospital networks or devices requires a separate written agreement covering data protection, security review, and clinical governance before such work begins.
5. **Return or destroy.** On written request, return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information, except one archival copy each Party must keep to comply with law or this Agreement.
6. **Notify.** Tell the other Party promptly if it becomes aware of any unauthorised disclosure or use.

## 5. Term

This Agreement takes effect on the date of the last signature and runs for **two (2) years**, unless terminated earlier by either Party on 30 days' written notice. Confidentiality obligations survive termination for the remaining term and, for any Confidential Information still protected, continue for three (3) years after termination. Return-or-destroy obligations survive until performed.

## 6. Exceptions to obligations

Section 4 does not apply to information the receiving Party must disclose:

1. that falls within any exception in Section 3;
2. when required by law, regulation, court order, or a regulator — provided the receiving Party gives the disclosing Party prompt written notice where lawful, so the disclosing Party can seek protection; or
3. to a Party's legal or financial advisers under Section 4.2 conditions.

## 7. No licence

Nothing in this Agreement grants any licence or other right to either Party's intellectual property. All rights in Confidential Information remain with the disclosing Party. Any use of the Nightingale prototype beyond evaluation, and any pilot or commercial arrangement, requires a separate written agreement.

## 8. No obligation to proceed

This Agreement does not oblige either Party to enter any further agreement, run a pilot, or continue discussions. Either Party may stop the evaluation at any time.

## 9. Governing law

## 10. Entire agreement and amendments

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on confidentiality for the Purpose and replaces any earlier understanding. Any change must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
